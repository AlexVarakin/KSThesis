Fixate the Center

Each trial begins with a central plus sign (+). Keep your eyes centered on the cross until the letter search display appears.

Search for the Target "T"

A rotated T (tilted either 90° to the left or 90° to the right) is hidden among rotated L's. Find it as quickly and accurately as possible.

3

Respond Promptly

Press the corresponding keyboard key as soon as you spot the target T:
Left Arrow for T tilted left, or Right Arrow for T tilted right.

4

Feedback & Next Trial

A brief sound will play after each response. If an incorrect response is made, the central fixation cross will turn red during the interval before the next trial.
